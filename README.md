# GlowPath

`glowpath:\` is a calm, private mood diary that lives in Telegram and on the web.

Log how you feel in a few taps, add a note, a photo or a voice message, and watch your days turn into color. GlowPath helps you notice what lifts you up and what drags you down, one day at a time.

**Try it:** https://mood-tracker-l17s.onrender.com

<!-- Hero GIF: logging an entry in the Telegram Mini App -->

## Why GlowPath

- **Color at the core.** Every mood has its own color, and each day in your diary is painted with a soft blend of the moods you lived through. A week of your life reads at a glance.
- **Made for Telegram.** Open the app right from the bot and sign in with one tap, no extra accounts needed.
- **Private by default.** Your entries, photos and notes belong only to you.

<!-- GIF: day colors in the feed and calendar -->

## What you can do

- **Log your mood** several times a day on a 1–5 scale you can rename, recolor and give your own icons.
- **Write notes** with formatting and reusable templates, and add up to five photos and a voice message.
- **Tag your day** with activities, weather, sleep and anything else you care about.
- **Track goals** and see how they connect to how you feel.
- **Look back** with a calendar, a feed of past days, monthly stats and a monthly recap.
- **Browse a gallery** of every photo from your diary.
- **Keep a streak** and unlock achievements for showing up.
- **Collect icon packs** from the in-app market.
- **Use it in English or Russian**, with your own time zone.
