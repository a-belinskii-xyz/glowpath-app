> A calm little space to check in with yourself. 🌈

GlowPath is a private mood diary that lives in **Telegram and on the web**.

Take a few seconds to log how you feel, write down what happened, add a photo or voice message — and slowly build a picture of your everyday life.

No pressure to write every day. No complicated journaling rituals.
Just your days, your colors, and the patterns you start noticing along the way. ✨

👉 **[Try GlowPath](https://mood-tracker-l17s.onrender.com)**

---

## 🌈 Your days, in color

GlowPath turns your mood into something you can actually *see*.

Each mood has its own color, and your diary gradually becomes a soft visual map of your days. Open the calendar or feed and you can instantly see how your weeks have been feeling.

Sometimes a pattern is easier to notice when you can see it.

## ✨ Made for real life

You don't need to write a perfect journal entry.

A few taps are enough to capture a moment — whether it's a great day, a rough one, or something in between.

* **Track your mood** several times a day with a 1–5 scale. Rename moods, change their colors and choose your own icons.
* **Capture the moment** with notes, formatting, reusable templates, up to five photos and a voice message.
* **Add context** with tags for activities, weather, sleep and anything else that matters to you.
* **Set goals** and see how your habits relate to the way you feel.
* **Look back** through your calendar, diary feed, monthly statistics and monthly recaps.
* **Keep your memories together** in a dedicated photo gallery.
* **Build a streak** and collect achievements simply by showing up.
* **Make it yours** with icon packs from the in-app market.
* **Use it your way** with English or Russian and your own time zone.

## 💙 Private by default

Your diary is personal.

GlowPath is designed around the idea that your moods, thoughts, photos and little everyday moments should belong to **you**.

No social feed.
No followers.
No pressure to share.

Just a space to check in with yourself.

## 📱 Built for Telegram

GlowPath is available directly through Telegram, so getting started takes just a tap.

Open the bot, launch the app and start tracking — without creating another account or remembering another password.
